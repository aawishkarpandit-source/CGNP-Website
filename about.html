About our company

A company that understands business.

Celta Group Digital (CG.NP Digital) is the digital division of Celta Group. We provide websites, software and digital services for businesses, organizations and individuals — practical solutions that are easy to use and dependable.

Reliable and accessible software for every business.

To build reliable, modern and accessible software solutions that help businesses grow and operate digitally.

To combine technology, creativity and business understanding to develop practical digital products for modern organizations.
